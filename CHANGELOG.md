# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.2.3

- The Mods tab page warns that 3x and 4x can break the scene changes in dialogue (the background may
  turn black), and that 2x or less is safe. The cause is being looked into; the speeds are unchanged.

## 1.2.2

- The Config page is built with the loader's `rq.config.settings`: the same checkbox and rows,
  kept under the same keys, and a **Reset** button (off, 2x everywhere). The area names no longer
  dim while the mod is off. Needs loader 2.35.0.

## 1.2.1

- On a quest's map, the F8 note shows under the encounter bar, centered on the 3D view, not over
  the bar.

## 1.2.0

- A Game Speed page in Config's Mods tab, with loader 1.8.0 or newer.

## 1.1.0

- A 1.5x step between 1x and 2x.

## 1.0.0

First version.

- A faster game clock for exploration, transitions, fights and level ups, each with its own factor.
- F8 turns it on and off (not F4, the game's Quests tab key). The F3 Game Speed page steps each area.
